# Introduction to AbleSet

Welcome to AbleSet!

Getting started using the app is simple. AbleSet lives as a little icon in your
menu bar. Click on it to open the main window.

To open the AbleSet interface on your computer, click "Open AbleSet" in the menu
bar window. Your browser will open with the local address of your AbleSet. If
you want to open AbleSet on your phone or tablet, make sure they are on the same
network as your computer and open the same address in their browser.

You can always see the address of AbleSet in its menu bar window:

![AbleSet Menu Bar Window](https://ableset.com/images/docs/01-introduction/ableset-menubar@2x.png)

AbleSet uses Ableton's locators to convert your session into a
setlist. The easiest way to set up a setlist is to place a locator at the
beginning of each track and give it the song's name. If you want to know more
about what you can do with locators in AbleSet, check out the
[Locator Notation](https://ableset.com/docs/locator-notation) documentation.
