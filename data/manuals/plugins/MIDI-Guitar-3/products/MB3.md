# MIDI Bass 3

# Make your bass sound like anything

[**MIDI Bass 3**](https://jam.live/products/MB3/) transforms your bass into a polyphonic MIDI controller. It listens to and recognizes what you're playing on your instrument then generates MIDI output to drive synthesizers — all in real time while using state-of-the-art audio tracking models optimized for bass players.

---

# No special pickup required

Unlike most bass synthesis devices, **MIDI Bass 3** simply requires the audio signal from your instrument's 1/4" jack (via a standard USB audio interface) to generate MIDI/MPE output.

---

# MIDI Polyphonic Expression (MPE)

With bass-to-MPE finally here, every note now has four dimensions of expression (strike, pressure, brightness and pitch). Multidimensional expressivity keeps the player more closely connected to the sound of the virtual instrument.

---

# Modular and extensible

**MIDI Bass 3** is a powerful, versatile, easy-to-use, modular system, that brings together third-party plugins, virtual instruments and MIDI controllers into a package that is easy for bassists to use, in the studio and on the stage.

---

JAM = Joint Audio + MIDI

All **Jam Origin** plugins integrate with one another as native modules and extensions.
